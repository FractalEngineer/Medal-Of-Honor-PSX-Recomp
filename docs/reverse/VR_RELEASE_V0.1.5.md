This update brings the original HUD into VR, with displays anchored to your hands and always facing you.

- **Hand-anchored HUD:** health and compass follow the left hand; ammo and weapon icon follow the right.
- **Original appearance preserved:** uses the game's native artwork, fonts, and number formatting.
- **Always readable:** HUD panels face your head without flipping as you rotate your wrists.
- **Tracking fallback:** the corresponding original HUD returns when a hand loses tracking.
- **Enabled by default** in the VDXR, SteamVR, and Oculus launchers.
- Added a desktop spectator mirror for watching the VR view.
- Added tracked weapon support for the Luger pistol.
- Increased smooth-turn speed by 20%.
- Disabled the newly introduced dithering effect in VR to preserve the clean image.

### Updated controls

- **Left stick click:** jump.
- **Right stick click:** toggle crouch.

Launch using the `.bat` matching your OpenXR runtime: `RunVR-VDXR.bat`, `RunVR-SteamVR.bat`, or `RunVR-Oculus.bat`.
